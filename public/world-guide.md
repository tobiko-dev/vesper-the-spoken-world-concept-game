# Vesper — The Spoken World: World Guide

## Lantern Vale, Year 430

Chapter I, **The Quiet Between Bells**, begins in Bellwether at the foot of the Academy road. The world treats spoken magic as structure: the declaration of a spell's level and name is part of the spell itself.

### Spell levels

Spell levels run from **1 to 10**. They describe the magic, not the person casting it. A Level 1 spell can be refined to extraordinary efficiency; knowing a higher-level spell does not automatically make someone a stronger or wiser mage.

Adventurer ranks are a separate ladder: **E → A → S → SS**.

### Incantations and direct casting

Long incantations help a caster stabilize and shape a spell. Exceptional mages can cast without the full incantation, but the declaration still matters: **“Level 1. Tinder.”** Parallel casting is a separate, rare achievement beyond ordinary incantation-less casting.

## Chapter I spellbook

The prototype hotbar contains nine learnable spells. Confirmed grimoire entries include **Tinder, Mend, Stoneward, Gale, Ember Lance, Rime, Flame Edge,** and **Thunder**, progressing from basic utility to advanced combat magic. The ninth slot is the chapter's first falling-star class spell.

**Ember Lance** is a Level 2 Ember spell with a mana cost of 25 and 3.5 second recovery. Its invocation is:

> “Goddess of fire, gather your scattered embers into a single spear. Level 2. Ember Lance.”

**Stoneward** is a Level 1 defensive spell lasting six seconds with no target requirement:

> “Patient earth, stand between this fragile heart and harm. Level 1. Stoneward.”

## Bellwether and the wider vale

- **Bellwether** — first-bell town and the start of the Academy road.
- **Hushwood** — a forest where sound behaves strangely.
- **Northwatch** — a cold frontier settlement built around old wards.
- **Sunken Choir** — ruins beneath the vale where silence has weight.

**Ilyra, Keeper of the waylights**, waits near Bellwether's central plaza. The first chronicle objective is **A light worth keeping**: wake the three waylights on the academy road.

## Future grimoire lore

The following entries are lore-only and intentionally locked in Chapter I:

- Casting techniques: **Parallel Cast → Multicast → Triple Cast → Quad Cast**
- Level 5: **Magic Jammer**
- Level 7: **Perfect Wall, Chaos Weed, Evil Flare, Icing Titan**
- Level 8: **Teleport, Thor's Hammer, Perfect Dispel**
- Level 9: **Thor's Lance, X-Gravity**
- Level 10: **Caldris, Vaelith**

Level 10 spells are mythic and are commonly named after their creators, especially the magic emperors associated with an element. Caldris is tied to scale-explosion magic; Vaelith to absorbing lightning and turning it into god-slaying force.

## Inspiration note

The spellcasting progression is inspired in part by *Rakudai Kenja no Gakuin Musou: Nidome no Tensei, S-Rank Cheat Majutsushi Boukenroku*. Vesper uses its own setting, characters, story structure, presentation, and original spell-lore extensions.
